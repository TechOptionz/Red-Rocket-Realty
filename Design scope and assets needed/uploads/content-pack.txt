Buyer guide content (condensed from the six tips)
SOURCE: https://redrocketrealty.com.au/tips-for-buyers/
Decide how much you want to spend. Start from comfortable weekly repayments. Ask a bank how much you can borrow and what repayments would be, so you are qualified before you look. Over-stretching can force an early sale at a loss.
The deposit. Banks generally look for 10% to 20%, with about 5% as genuine personal savings. A 20% deposit usually avoids lenders mortgage insurance. Lenders also assess savings pattern and job security. Family may help make up the balance.
Choose your suburbs. Pick two or three; more makes price comparison confusing. Weigh price range, schools, shops, public transport and distance from family.
Purchase price. Check you are paying fair market value: buy recent sales data or ask the agent for comparable sales. A fair-priced home can beat a "bargain" that needs costly renovation. Make a reasonable offer and allow for finance, building and pest inspections and settlement timing.
Use a building and pest inspector. Arrange an inspection when you sign. Use a registered inspector and attend the last half hour to go through the report. NEEDS VERIFICATION: the tip cites the "QBSA" and a "gold card"; the Queensland regulator is now the QBCC, so this wording is out of date.
Use a solicitor. They protect your interests, explain the contract and run searches such as title, council and main roads.

Seller tips (condensed)
SOURCE: https://redrocketrealty.com.au/tips-for-sellers/
Be sure the price is right (pricing). Overpricing scares buyers off. Research local sales: what sold, for how much and how long it took, to find true market value.
Book an inspection (preparation). An early inspection shows likely repairs, so the seller chooses who does the work and negotiates the cost.
Cleaning for cash (presentation). Touch up paint, tidy gardens, fix cracked windows, polish and scrub. A little work lifts the price.
Premium promotion (marketing). Use several channels for fast exposure: high-resolution photos inside and out, professional signage, open homes and printed brochures.
Bring out the best in your property (presentation). Accentuate unique features and create a feel visitors remember. The seller is selling a dream, not just a property.
